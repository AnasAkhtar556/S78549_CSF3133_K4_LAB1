Tech Innovation Summit 2026

Who We Are

Welcome to the official portal of the annual Tech Innovation Summit. We are a dedicated platform hosted by the Faculty of Computer Science & Information Technology to foster learning, collaboration, and advancement in cutting-edge technologies.

Our Mission

Our mission is to bridge the gap between academia and industry. We empower students, researchers, and tech enthusiasts by providing access to world-class keynote speakers, insightful panel discussions, and valuable networking opportunities.

What We Offer

  • Interactive Workshops: Hands-on sessions covering the latest tools and frameworks in artificial intelligence, software engineering, and cybersecurity.
  • Paper Presentations: A stage for emerging researchers to showcase groundbreaking findings.
  • Industry Networking: Opportunities to connect directly with tech leaders, hiring managers, and academic experts.
